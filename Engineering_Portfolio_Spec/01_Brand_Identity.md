# 6. Avoided Brand Direction


Do NOT create:

## Gaming Style

Avoid:

- Cyberpunk overload
- Neon everywhere
- RGB gaming colors


## Cartoon Style

Avoid:

- Cute drone
- Rounded toy appearance
- Illustration style


## Startup Template Style

Avoid:

- Generic gradient hero
- Stock illustrations
- Standard cards
