# 05 - Content Strategy

# Engineering Storytelling Strategy


---

# 1. Purpose


The portfolio should not simply display achievements.


It should communicate:


"How an engineer thinks, builds, and solves problems."


Every project should answer:

Why did I build this?

What challenge did I face?

How did I solve it?

What did I learn?

---

# 2. Personal Story Structure


The website narrative should follow:

Curiosity

↓

Learning

↓

Building

↓

Competing

↓

Improving

↓

Exploring Globally

↓

Future Engineering

This creates an engineering growth story.


---

# 3. Content Tone


The writing style should be:


## Professional


Use:

- Technical explanation
- Engineering terminology
- Clear results


Avoid:

- Excessive self-promotion
- Empty words


---

## Personal


Show:

- Challenges
- Mistakes
- Improvements


Engineering is not only success.

Engineering is problem solving.


---

# 4. About Section Content


## Main Introduction


Recommended:

I am Yu Jen Lin,
an Intelligent Automation Engineering student
focused on robotics, embedded systems,
and intelligent machines.

I enjoy transforming ideas into real systems
through hardware, software, and engineering design.

---

# 5. Engineering Philosophy


Section:


## My Engineering Belief


Content direction:

Build.
Test.
Fail.
Improve.

Every prototype is a step toward
a better solution.

---

# 6. Project Writing Framework


Every project follows:


## 01. Introduction


What is this project?


Example:

An autonomous robotic system developed
for competition environments.

---

## 02. Challenge


What problem existed?


Example:

The robot required stable movement,
accurate control,
and reliable mechanical structure.

---

## 03. Engineering Solution


Explain:


- Hardware design
- Software architecture
- Mechanical decisions


---

## 04. Result


Show:


- Competition result
- Performance improvement
- Technical achievement


---

## 05. Reflection


Explain:


What was learned?


This creates authenticity.


---

# 7. Technical Content Balance


Avoid:


Only technical:

Arduino
Motor
Sensor
Code

Only story:

I learned many things.

Use:

Problem

Engineering Solution

Technical Detail

Result

---

# 8. Recommended Content Priority


Priority order:


## Level 1

Major Engineering Projects


Examples:


- Rocket Project
- Robotics Competition
- AI Project


---

## Level 2

Technical Development


Examples:


- OpenCV
- Embedded Systems
- Automation


---

## Level 3

Learning Experience


Examples:


- International Exchange
- Workshops
- Courses


---

# 9. Personal Branding Through Content


The final impression:


Not:


"I joined many activities."


Instead:


"I continuously develop engineering capability through real-world projects."


---

# 10. Final Content Principle


Every section should strengthen one message:

I build intelligent systems
by combining engineering,
technology, and creativity.
